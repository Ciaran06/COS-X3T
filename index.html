What it understands

Saying where you are
reg number one two G one two three four five → the vehicle 12-G-12345
van 191 D 12345 · location back yard · inside store · outside store · container
Then the spot inside it: shelf number A1 · shelf A2 · bay three · rack two · next spot
When you're finished: done the back yard · finished here — it closes that location and asks for the next.
Counting
coke twenty bottles · twenty bottles of coke · ten cases of coke
Packs convert on the item's pack size, so ten cases of Coke lands as 240 bottles and is shown both ways.
Drums and part reels
drum A B C D one, eight hundred metres — the drum ID is kept with the length, so part drums stay identifiable.
twenty full drums of ninety six fibre multiplies by the standard length on the item.
Fixing and checking
undo / scratch that removes the last line · total reads back the running total.

Speech recognition runs on the device, which is why it keeps working with no signal. A production build loads your part codes as a boosted vocabulary so codes and contractor names are recognised first time.
